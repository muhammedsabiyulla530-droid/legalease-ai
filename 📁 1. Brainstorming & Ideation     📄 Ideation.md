## Problem Identified

Creating legal documents can be difficult for people who do not have knowledge of legal language and document formats.

## Proposed Solution

LegalEase is an AI-powered application that helps users generate structured legal document drafts based on the information provided by the user.

## Objectives

- Make legal document drafting easier.
- Save time in preparing documents.
- Provide a simple user interface.
- Generate structured legal document drafts.
- Allow users to download the generated document.

## Main Features

- Legal document type selection
- User information input
- AI-powered document generation
- Document preview
- Document download
- Simple user interface

## Target Users

- Students
- Individuals
- Small businesses
- Organizations
- People who need basic legal document drafts

## Disclaimer

LegalEase is an AI-assisted document drafting tool and does not replace professional legal advice.
