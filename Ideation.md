## Idea

Legal documents are often difficult for ordinary people to understand and prepare. 
Many people do not know the correct format, important information, or structure required 
for common legal documents.

LegalEase is an AI-powered application designed to help users generate simple and 
structured legal document drafts based on the information provided by the user.

## Problem Identified

Users may face difficulties when:

- Understanding legal terminology
- Preparing legal documents
- Knowing what information should be included
- Following a proper document format
- Creating documents quickly

## Proposed Solution

LegalEase uses Artificial Intelligence to generate a structured legal document draft 
from the user's requirements.

The system collects relevant information from the user and uses an AI model to create 
a suitable document draft.

## Main Objectives

- Make legal document drafting easier
- Provide a simple user interface
- Generate structured document drafts
- Reduce the time required for preparing documents
- Help users understand the generated content
- Provide downloadable document output

## Target Users

- Students
- Individuals
- Small businesses
- Organizations
- People who need basic document drafts

## Important Note

LegalEase is an AI-assisted document drafting tool. It does not replace a qualified 
lawyer or provide professional legal advice. Users should consult a qualified legal 
professional when necessary.
